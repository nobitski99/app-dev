# Vikings(TV Series)
*Vikings* historical drama television series created and written by Michael Hirst. The show is inspired by the sagas of Viking Ragnar Lothbrok, one of the best-known Legendary Norse Heroes
# Key Characters
**Ragnar Lothbrok** - A legendary Norse hero who rises from farmer to a fearless warrior and king.
**Lagertha** - A fierce shieldmaiden and Ragnar's first wife.
**Bjorn Ironside** - Ragnar and Lagertha's ambitious eldest son.
# Themes
**Exploration and Conquest** The expansion of Norse territories into England and France.
**Clash of Cultures** The intense religious conflict between Norse paganism and Christianity.
